## 4. Where the paper differs from the plan's row M (the plan is not changed here; each is brought to Joe)

1. **The treatment rule.** The plan's row: "a utility is treated from the first year a data center above 5 MW enters a county its territory touches". The paper sums newly activated capacity over a county's facilities each year and keeps county-years above 5 MW (p. 18), so a county can be treated by several facilities each 5 MW or smaller. This is the difference D-104 recorded from the record; the paper confirms it. The M4 step's wording ("in place of any site above 5 MW") rests on the plan's reading. Until Joe rules, the counting stays a required argument with no default.
2. **Sample rules the plan's row omits.** Utilities that ever file the Short Form are excluded (p. 15); pairs must serve residential, commercial and industrial customers and have "a retail component" (p. 21); pairs first treated before 2010 are dropped (p. 20). The plan's row states none of them. The first is settled and the second and third are settled in part (rows 17 to 19); the fourth is already coded.
3. **Wording only.** "A county its territory touches" against "the counties it serves in that state" (p. 18). The EIA-861 territory is a county list, so the agent reads no difference in substance; the "in that state" part is implied by the plan's utility-by-state unit.
4. **Numbers.** None differ. The plan's 5.2% is the residential investor-owned figure; the abstract's 5.6% is the investor-owned figure over all customer classes (Table 3 col. 1, p. 48).
