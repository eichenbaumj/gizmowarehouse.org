## Where the paper differs from the plan's row C text

1. **Fiber blocks.** The plan's "Instrument inputs" says blocks are "placed in HIFLD territories by their internal point". The paper counts blocks that "intersect" each territory, after resolving overlapping polygons to the smallest (S1.1, p. 25; S1.5, p. 34). The plan does not claim its method is the paper's, but row C's paper specification uses the paper's.
2. **"Does not affect an instrumented estimate".** The plan says the scale of our shares is unresolved and that this does not affect an instrumented estimate. That holds for a uniform rescaling only. The read shows the paper's construction differs from ours in overlap resolution and block placement, which changes shares unit by unit, not uniformly. Our shares sum to 205% of the national total across units; the paper's territories are mutually exclusive. The agent's reading is that this can move the instrumented estimate.
3. **December filings.** The plan's December Form 477 choice is not stated in the paper, which says "annually". Not a contradiction; the choice is ours.
4. Every printed number the plan's row C carries matches the paper (0.182 and 0.034, 59 for 59.43, 0.061 for 0.0610 and 0.018, 0.110, 0.150, 0.236, 0.155 and 0.028), and the smallest-overlapping-polygon rule in C0 is the paper's.
