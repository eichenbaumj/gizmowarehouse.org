## Contradictions between the paper and the plan's row U text (the plan is not changed here)

1. Row U, step U0: "(the tie rule is ours; they do not state one)". The paper states part of a rule for overlapping polygons, strict containment with retail providers over wholesale-only territories (p. 5). What it does not state is how it chose among several retail polygons. The smallest-polygon part is ours; the retail priority is theirs.
2. Row U, "Their specification": "no customer-count floor". True as far as it goes, but the paper excludes Short Form filers (endnote 10, p. 17), which removes about 560 small utilities, and drops utilities seen in fewer than 3 years (p. 6). The plan states neither rule.
3. Row U, "Their specification": "log residential price (bundled revenue over bundled sales ...)". The paper says revenue over sales for bundled-service utilities (p. 6). It does not say the revenue and sales are the bundled columns. Not a contradiction in substance, but the plan states more than the paper does.
4. Row U, "Their numbers": "pooled 0.0095 (SE 0.0026) without controls, 0.0094 with". Correct; the paper also prints 0.0026 as the with-controls error (p. 23), which the plan omits. Not a contradiction.
5. The plan's 2026-11-19 status paragraph lists "what 'indistinguishable' means at U1" among elements the paper leaves unstated. Confirmed: the paper has no test of differences between ownership coefficients.
